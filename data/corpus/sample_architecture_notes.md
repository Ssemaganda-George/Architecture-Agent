# Sample Architecture Corpus

This corpus contains high-level architectural principles for sustainable, mixed-use, and resilient building design.

## Sustainability
- Orient buildings for passive solar gain.
- Use massing strategies to reduce operational energy.
- Specify low-carbon materials: cross-laminated timber, recycled steel, high-performance glazing.

## Mixed Use
- Separate retail, office, and residential zones with layered vertical circulation.
- Provide shared amenity spaces for community and co-working.
- Design adaptable floorplates for future program changes.

## Resilience
- Prioritize flood-resilient ground floors and raised services.
- Integrate renewable energy systems and battery storage.
- Use modular facade systems to support phased construction.
